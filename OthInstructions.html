Othello Instructions

Othello, also known as Reversi, is an easy-to-play strategy game. It uses an 8x8 board and 64 discs, each with one black side and one white side. Each player controls one color, and the goal is to finish with more of your color showing on the board than your opponent has.

Gameplay

The board begins with two black pieces and two white pieces placed in the center. Players then take turns placing one piece of their own color on an empty square. The first player is randomly selected.

Capturing

Capture opponent pieces by placing one of your pieces so that one or more opponent pieces are trapped in a straight horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line between your new piece and another one of your pieces. The trapped pieces are then flipped to your color, increasing the number of your pieces on the board.

Example: You have the black pieces. One black piece is already on the board, followed by four white pieces in a line. If you place a black piece at the other end of that line, the four white pieces are trapped between two black pieces and turn black.

Making a Move

Every move you make must capture at least one opponent piece. On your turn, place one of your pieces next to a straight line of one or more opponent pieces, with another one of your pieces at the far end of that line. It sounds complicated, but available move squares are shown with semi-transparent circles, so the game is easy to learn once you start playing.

If no available move on the board captures any pieces, you must pass and your opponent gets another turn. If both players pass in a row, there are no more legal moves and the game ends.

Winning

The game ends when the board is full, when both players pass in a row, or when one player surrenders. The pieces on the board are then counted, and the player with the most pieces wins.
