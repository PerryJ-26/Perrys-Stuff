Acey-Deucey

The Sailor's Backgammon

Rules

Acey-deucey plays like backgammon with one big twist: no checkers start on the board. All 15 of each player's checkers begin on the bar, and you enter them one at a time as the game unfolds — just like re-entering after being hit. Move your checkers around the board and bear them all off first to win.

The special rule that gives the game its name: roll a 1 and a 2 and you play both of those numbers as usual, then call any double you like (say, double 5s) and play all four of those moves too — then you get to roll again for a completely fresh turn. It's the best roll in the game.
