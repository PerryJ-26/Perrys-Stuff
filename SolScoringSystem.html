Solitaire Scoring System

This methodology applies to all Game Hub solitaire games.

Calculating Scores

The starting score equals: −52 points for games with one deck; −104 points for games with two decks.

5 points are awarded for each card properly built on the foundations.

Example: If you play Klondike (1 deck = 52 cards), you start with a score of −52; when you add a card to a foundation, the score is increased by 5 points (−47, −42, −37, etc.).

The highest possible score is 208 points for one deck and 416 for two decks.

Recorded Scores

The Recorded Scores will be displayed in ranking order of the highest score, the least number of moves, and the lowest time. This combination will eliminate the chance of a tie score.

Example: If there are two scores with 208 points and one score has 117 moves and the second has 127 moves, the first score will be above the second score on the scorecard. If they also have the same number of moves, the score with the lowest time will be above the other score.
