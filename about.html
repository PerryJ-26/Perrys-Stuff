About

As most of you know, I'm a retired programmer with time on my hands. I wanted to exercise my brain, so I started doing Word Search Puzzles (now I'm addicted). I desired something more stimulating, so I built a few spreadsheets and polished my library book database program which is 35 years old and contains almost 10,000 books. I ran out of ideas to occupy myself, so I decided to learn how to design and develop websites, a skill that I don't have any experience with. I'll never be as good as my brother Peter or Claude, an AI Tool, but it sure is fun to learn something new!

With help from Peter who provided his Web for Business course and Claude, I was able to develop this website. Claude helped with coding support while I shaped the design, functionality, and user experience. I've tried to make the games as realistic as possible and attempted to apply the same logic that humans would use.

My next step is to develop a webpage without Claude's help. I'll post it here when it's done.

Tic Tac Toe was the first game we created and was an excellent way to begin developing my skills. It has the option to play against AI or a real person.

Yahtzee includes bonuses and stores the high scores for everyone to see.

Acey Deucey is a variation of Backgammon that is very popular with sailors. It also has the option to play against AI or a real person.

Pinochle has three AI players. The logic behind playing Pinochle is complicated, so I added 3 options to assist the player in learning the game.

Enjoy!
